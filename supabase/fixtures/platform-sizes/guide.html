What size works everywhere?

  • 1080 x 1080 pixels (1:1): most feeds
  • 1080 x 1350 pixels (4:5): more room on a phone
  • 1080 x 1920 pixels (9:16): Stories

Facebook shared timeline image guidelines

    • Square images: 1080×1080 pixels (1:1 aspect ratio)

Instagram feed and carousel image guidelines
